About the Program

Computing is a Life Skill. Every Child Deserves It.

The Digital Literacy Program is CLT India's initiative to bring structured digital learning resources to marginalized communities. We provide comprehensive, in-house developed curriculum that spans two levels — Foundation and Advanced — giving students hands-on experience with technology regardless of their background.

Our program reaches students who have dropped out of school as well as those currently pursuing their education, ensuring no learner is left behind in the digital transition. By bridging the access gap, we support holistic development and open pathways to further education and livelihoods.

Aligned with the Digital India vision, the program equips beneficiaries with both technical proficiency and soft skills, creating a foundation for meaningful participation in the digital economy.

The program welcomes young students who are either currently enrolled in high school, PUC, or degree programs — or who have dropped out and are looking for a way back into learning and opportunity.
