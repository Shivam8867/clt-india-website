What is e-Patashale?

Digital STEM Education for Rural Classrooms

While educational technology is increasingly being adopted by well-resourced private schools, CLT is focused on extending the benefits of technology-enabled learning to government schools. The CLT e-Patashale initiative was conceptualized as a transformative model to enhance learning experiences and bring meaningful change to rural classrooms.

At the heart of the e-Patashale model, CLT master teachers drive innovation by creating localized STEM digital content and using accessible technology platforms to transform teaching practices at the community level.

The sustainablity of the program is ensured by equipping teachers with quality resources and enhanced teaching skills. Offline digital content and solar-powered systems help overcome connectivity and electricity challenges, ensuring uninterrupted access to learning.

The digital STEM content is for 5th–10th grades, based on NCERT syllabus. It has concept-based videos, real-time experiments, activity-based learning and assessments.

Digital Learning Content

Structured digital content for Science, Mathematics and English Grammar across Grades 5 to 10

Science

Science lessons bring the curriculum to life with clear concept explanations, hands-on experiments and regular assessments — making even the toughest topics easy to understand for every learner from Class 5 to 10.

Mathematics

Mathematics lessons build strong foundations with concept-based video explanations, solved examples and practice problems, backed by chapter-wise assessments that build confidence and speed for Class 5 to 10.

English Grammar

Grammar lessons take learners from the basics in Class 5 to advanced concepts by Class 10 — covering parts of speech, tenses, voice, clauses and writing skills, all explained simply in every video.
